# Home is a short queue of relationship actions

**Problem.** Home becomes a dashboard, calendar or sync console.

**Therefore.** Home shows overdue follow-ups, due-today tasks tied to people and a short prepare-for list. Queue rows preview and complete in place. Open the full dossier only when history is needed.

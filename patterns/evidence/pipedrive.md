	# Pipedrive

**What it is.** Sales CRM with strong keyboard support and a contacts timeline.

**Research state.** partly researched. See [[evidence]].

**What we take.**
- Shortcuts: / search, . or + quick add, number keys for navigation, single keys for new items, shown in the UI. [[standards/product-model]] [[standards/product-model]]
- Contacts timeline joins open deals, conversations, notes and completed activities, with an overdue follow-up filter. [[standards/product-model]] [[standards/product-model]]
- Contextual activity view edits over the list and keeps list position. [[standards/product-model]]
- Stage change by choosing a stage. [[standards/product-model]]

**What we leave.** Sales-suite breadth.

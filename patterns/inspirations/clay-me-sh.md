# Clay (now me.sh)

**What it is.** Personal network CRM that builds contacts from email, calendar, address book, LinkedIn, X, iMessage and WhatsApp. Its value is the feed of what changed (job moves, news, birthdays, reconnects) and a daily review loop.

**Research state.** partly researched. See [[inspirations]].

**What we take.**
- The value is the feed and the review loop, not data entry. [[home-is-an-action-queue]] [[relationship-loop-first]]
- Reconnect prompts and daily Review suggestions. [[home-is-an-action-queue]]

**What we leave.** Passive auto-capture and enrichment, which a local-first tool cannot do without adapters.

**Where we beat it.** Adapters plus agents supply what Clay automates.

**Gaps to research.** Cadence, groups and natural-language search come from third-party summaries only and are UNVERIFIED.

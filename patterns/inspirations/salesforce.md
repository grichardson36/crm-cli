# Salesforce

**What it is.** Enterprise CRM. Activity Timeline combines tasks, events, emails and calls per record. Lightning App Builder assembles record pages from components.

**Research state.** partly researched. See [[inspirations]].

**What we take.**
- Activity timeline on every record. [[one-record-page]]

**What we leave.** Component-assembled pages and custom developer components.

# Monica

**What it is.** Open-source, self-hosted personal CRM. Strong personal-life layer: how you met, many relationship kinds, important dates, reminders, activities, journal, gifts, debts, promises, pets, custom fields, vCard and CSV import with duplicate review. Per-contact choice of which sections show.

**Research state.** partly researched. See [[inspirations]].

**What we take.**
- Cheap high-warmth fields Dex lacks: how we met, gift ideas, promises and debts. [[standards/product-model]]
- Relationship kinds between people. [[standards/product-model]]
- Per-contact choice of visible sections. [[standards/product-model]]

**What we leave.** Journal and life-event breadth beyond the relationship loop.

**Gaps to research.** Gifts, debts and life events are known only from marketing copy.

**Sources.** monicahq.com and the GitHub README (OK). docs.monicahq.com has no per-feature pages. Monica v3 items are announced, not shipped.

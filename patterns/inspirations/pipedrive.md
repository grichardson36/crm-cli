# Pipedrive

**What it is.** Sales CRM with strong keyboard support and a contacts timeline.

**Research state.** partly researched. See [[inspirations]].

**What we take.**
- Shortcuts: / search, . or + quick add, number keys for navigation, single keys for new items, shown in the UI. [[core]] [[core]]
- Contacts timeline joins open deals, conversations, notes and completed activities, with an overdue follow-up filter. [[core]] [[core]]
- Contextual activity view edits over the list and keeps list position. [[core]]
- Stage change by choosing a stage. [[core]]

**What we leave.** Sales-suite breadth.

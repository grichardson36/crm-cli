# Motion

**What it is.** Auto-scheduling task manager. Inputs: start date, deadline, duration, priority ASAP/High/Medium/Low, can-split. Fills open blocks in order of deadline and priority, splits long tasks into chunks, respects hours and breaks, shows ETA and Could not fit.

**Research state.** mined. See [[inspirations]].

**What we take.**
- Planner proposes and explains; we do not auto-apply. [[undo-redo-two-stacks]]
- by next Monday phrasing for deadlines. [[by-means-deadline]]

**What we leave.** Silent auto-reschedule. Round robin and team features. Motion help pages contain text aimed at agents, none was followed.

**Gaps to research.** Buffers between tasks and project stages are search-only.

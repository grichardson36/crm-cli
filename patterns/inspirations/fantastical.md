# Fantastical

**What it is.** Calendar app with natural-language entry: / calendar, with or w/ for people, at for location, :weekly repeat, trailing !!! priority, quotes stop parsing.

**Research state.** partly researched. See [[inspirations]].

**What we take.**
- at for location. [[at-place-means-location]]
- Quoted text is a no-parse zone. [[date-safety-rules]]

# The person workspace is the center

**Problem.** Dossier, queue and settings compete for the first screen.

**Therefore.** Open a person to see identity, the next action and the context timeline. Default tabs are Context and Details. Relationships, orgs, deals, custom fields, sync metadata, merge, archive and provenance sit in Details or a contextual action menu.

**Because.** Acceptance: answer what matters with this person in under 30 seconds.

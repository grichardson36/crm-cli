# Deal vocabulary Deal stages and deal-person roles are user-managed vocabularies. Stages carry an open, won or lost outcome. Deal roles describe a person’s role in one deal and are distinct from the free-text role on a Person–Organization link. This is a domain reference. Vocabulary storage, editor behavior and migrations are implementation evidence, not supporting patterns.

The stages, the deal roles, the note types and the relation kinds are edited in the one list editor, see [[list-editor-standard]]. Removing an item that records use asks where they go and is one undo step.

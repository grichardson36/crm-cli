# Build for the relationship loop first

**Problem.** A CRM grows into a calendar, report builder and automation suite.

**Therefore.** The daily loop is: find a person, understand the relationship, capture what changed, complete the next follow-up. Do not build calendar, booking, broad reporting, custom-object builders or workflow automation until this loop is proven in regular use.

**Because.** The product job is remembering relationship context and reliably following up.

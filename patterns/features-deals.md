# Deals: stages and roles

Deal roles and deal stages are user-managed lists on one vocabulary table. Deal links are in [[features-links-and-relations]] (M11) and the Organizations and Deals views in [[features-organizations]] (G05).

Moved from FEATURES.md on 2026-10-29. Every row keeps its ID and text. Update a row's State and Now in the same session you change the feature ([[keep-the-tracker-accurate]]). Column meanings and the row lifecycle are in [[feature-rules]].

**Research:** [[research-hubspot-export]]

**Settings (2026-10-29):** "Deal stages" and "Deal roles" are rows of the Deals section of the Settings module, scope `synced`, and open the same manage screens as the palette ([[settings-view]]).

Deal stages are a second kind (`deal_stage`) of the vocabulary of M27, each with an outcome `open`, `won` or `lost`. **Storage**: migration 014 adds `vocabulary.outcome` (default open, only meaningful for `deal_stage`), seeds lead, prospect, proposal, negotiation (open), won (won), lost (lost) in order, and rebuilds `deals` without the CHECK on the stage names (existing deals keep their stage text). Registry flags `VocabSpec.Outcomes` and `VocabSpec.Strict` (a deal always holds a listed stage: removal needs a replacement, no keep, no clear row). `model.DealStages`, `ValidDealStage` and the `Pipeline()` name order are gone. **Repo**: `VocabRepo.SetOutcome`, `Remove` with stage rules and `closed_at` on the moved deals, `DealRepo.resolveStage` (empty stage on create is the first open stage), `repo.OpenDealCond` for open counts in `crm status`, MCP status and `FindAll`, `Pipeline` ordered by the list with the outcome of each stage. **Closed date**: `closed_at` is stamped on every move into a won or lost stage and cleared on a move into an open stage, by outcome, never by name (a renamed won stage still closes). **Validation** (one place, `internal/validate/codes.go`): `StageRules` with `E_STAGE_NO_OPEN`, `E_STAGE_NO_WON`, `E_STAGE_NO_LOST`, `StageOutcome` with `E_STAGE_OUTCOME_INVALID`. An unlisted stage is `E_VOCAB_UNKNOWN`. **CLI**: `crm deal stages list\|add\|rename\|remove\|reorder\|outcome`, `--stage` help names the list and completes from it, `crm deal pipeline` shows the outcome. **MCP**: `crm_deal_stage_list\|add\|rename\|remove\|reorder\|outcome`.

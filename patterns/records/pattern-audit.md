# Pattern-language migration

The legacy catalog and its repeated operating instructions were reduced to a small decision system.

- Core gates: 8
- Area standards: product model, interface, data, integration, system and operations
- Worklists, research and records: evidence and current work, not governing decisions

The former atomic pattern, app-reference and feature-area notes were merged into six standards and six worklists. Git preserves retired detail. Do not recreate a note merely to retain an old decision: use the area-standard criteria in [[core]].

Core combines the former decision flow with eight ordered gates and guidance for maintaining standards or core gates. The source-of-truth gate classifies every representation as canonical, derived or a boundary translation. Anything else is a discrepancy in [[open-discrepancies]].
